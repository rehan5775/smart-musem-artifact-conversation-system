# Identify Operations

| ID | Operation | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| OP01 | Perform Sensor Self-Check | Check whether all essential sensors are working correctly. |
| OP02 | Check Environmental-Control Devices | Verify that cooling, heating, humidifying, dehumidifying and other control devices are available. |
| OP03 | Record Artifact Identification | Record the artifact ID and relevant identification information. |
| OP04 | Load Artifact Environmental Limits | Load the permitted temperature, humidity and other environmental limits for the artifact. |
| OP05 | Monitor Environmental Conditions | Continuously monitor temperature, humidity, light and other chamber conditions. |
| OP06 | Check Chamber Door Status | Detect whether the chamber door is open or closed. |
| OP07 | Compare Temperature with Permitted Range | Compare the current temperature with the artifact's allowed range. |
| OP08 | Correct Abnormal Temperature | Adjust environmental controls when temperature goes outside the permitted range. |
| OP09 | Verify Temperature Recovery | Confirm that temperature has returned to the permitted range. |
| OP10 | Compare Humidity with Permitted Range | Compare the current humidity with the artifact's allowed range. |
| OP11 | Correct Abnormal Humidity | Adjust environmental controls when humidity goes outside the permitted range. |
| OP12 | Verify Humidity Recovery | Confirm that humidity has returned to the permitted range. |
| OP13 | Detect Significant Vibration | Detect vibration that may threaten the artifact. |
| OP14 | Suspend Risky Activities | Stop or pause activities that may cause further risk to the artifact. |
| OP15 | Verify Vibration Stabilization | Confirm that harmful vibration has stopped or reduced to a safe level. |
| OP16 | Reduce Light Exposure | Reduce lighting when excessive light may damage the artifact. |
| OP17 | Activate Additional Environmental Controls | Turn on extra control measures when normal controls are insufficient. |
| OP18 | Generate Operator Alert | Notify the responsible operator about abnormal or critical conditions. |
| OP19 | Switch to Emergency Power | Change to emergency power when the main power supply is unavailable. |
| OP20 | Record Power-Loss Incident | Log the power failure with relevant time and system information. |
| OP21 | Verify Chamber Safe Condition | Check that the chamber and artifact are in a safe condition after an incident. |
| OP22 | Authorize Artifact Removal | Confirm that required safety conditions are satisfied before artifact removal. |
